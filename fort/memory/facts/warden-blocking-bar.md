The Warden has a BLOCKING BAR: blocking is reserved for findings where merging
makes the fort worse than not merging. Everything else is APPROVE-WITH-FINDINGS
with the finding filed as a bead. Round 2 and later block only on regressions
and unfixed round-1 blockers; a third new blocker escalates to Justin instead.
