# The Naming of Longburn — 2026-08-03

The working name LONGBURN was established at founding by a naming survey (Claude subagent, full report preserved in the founding session). The name is the mechanic: a long burn is the interplanetary transfer — thrust committed, irreversible, followed by weeks of consequence. Two syllables, spellable after one hearing, works as icon, subreddit, and verb.

Validation at naming time: no Steam/itch/trademark collisions; GitHub org free; .io/.game/.net/.gg/.space showed available via whois (.com held since 2008); only real-world collision a New Zealand village of 330. Unverified: subreddit/handles (Reddit blocked checks); registrar checkout pending (bead longburn-8zw).

Names considered and killed by collision, for the record: **Ephemeris** (funded Steam space-4X), **Aphelion** (three Steam titles incl. DON'T NOD 2026), **Oldlight** (oldlight.io — a live game on nearly our thesis, two months ahead; see study bead), Syzygy, Coriolis. Backups if Longburn must ever be surrendered: Lightlag, Occultation.

The fort's own name is a separate matter, chosen by its founders at the moot.
